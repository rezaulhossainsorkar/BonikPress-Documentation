01 · Fundamental identity

What this provider actually owns.

The Storage & Data Provider is infrastructure for controlled access, retrieval, creation, modification, deletion, organization, integrity, and lifecycle of supported BonikPress data.

THE DEFINITION

Data-management infrastructure, not a database wrapper.

Its job is larger than issuing low-level database calls or WooCommerce methods. A data operation may require identification, initialization, validation, preparation, execution, verification, completion, state handling, failure handling, and result management.

THE BOUNDARY

Manage the data domain without absorbing other domains.

Core remains the ecosystem authority. Request Manager remains the communication authority. WooCommerce remains authoritative over WooCommerce product meaning. The Storage & Data Provider manages supported data operations inside its own boundary.

02 · Two data domains

Two domains. One provider. Different ownership semantics.

The architecture deliberately distinguishes data BonikPress works with from data BonikPress itself owns.

MAJOR ROLE 01

WooCommerce Product Data Management

Manages BonikPress’s controlled interaction with WooCommerce product data. WooCommerce remains the domain authority for product meaning; this provider supplies the infrastructure for operating on that data.

Product Identification

Identifies the WooCommerce product or products involved in a data operation, establishing what the operation is actually targeting.

Product Initialization

Establishes or prepares the product representation required for the operation before the actual data work begins.

Product Validation

Determines whether product data and the requested product operation satisfy the requirements needed to proceed safely.

Product Retrieval

Retrieves WooCommerce product data through appropriate WooCommerce mechanisms and makes the requested product information available to the consuming infrastructure.

Product Creation

Creates new WooCommerce products and manages the product information required during the creation process.

Product Modification

Updates existing WooCommerce product information while respecting the rules and conditions of the product-data domain.

Product Deletion

Removes WooCommerce products when a valid deletion operation has been requested and the necessary conditions have been satisfied.

Product Metadata Management

Manages supported metadata associated with WooCommerce products within the provider’s product-data boundary.

Product Relationship Management

Manages relevant relationships between products and other supported product-related information.

Product Operation Verification

Determines whether a product operation produced the expected result after execution.

MAJOR ROLE 02

BonikPress Ecosystem Data Management

Manages data owned by and required by the BonikPress ecosystem itself. Here BonikPress can be the authority over the persistent data being managed.

Ecosystem Data Identification

Identifies the specific BonikPress-owned data involved in an operation.

Ecosystem Data Initialization

Establishes the required representation or initial state for BonikPress-owned data.

Ecosystem Data Validation

Ensures that ecosystem data and requested operations satisfy defined requirements.

Ecosystem Data Retrieval

Retrieves BonikPress-owned data through the appropriate data mechanisms.

Ecosystem Data Creation

Creates new persistent BonikPress data and establishes the information required for its creation.

Ecosystem Data Modification

Updates existing BonikPress-owned data while preserving its defined structural and operational rules.

Ecosystem Data Deletion

Removes BonikPress-owned data when the deletion is valid and appropriate.

Ecosystem Data Structure Management

Manages the structural organization of BonikPress-owned data.

Ecosystem Data Relationship Management

Manages relationships between different pieces of BonikPress-owned data.

Ecosystem Data Operation Verification

Verifies that operations against ecosystem data produced valid results.

MAJOR ROLE 03

Data Operation Management

This role is about the operation itself, not a particular data domain. It provides the common lifecycle through which a data operation progresses.

Operation Identification

Determines what data operation is being requested.

Operation Initialization

Establishes the initial operational state and prerequisites.

Operation Validation

Determines whether the requested operation is valid and sufficiently prepared to proceed.

Operation Preparation

Prepares data, parameters, resources, and execution conditions.

Operation Execution

Coordinates execution of the actual data operation.

Operation Verification

Determines whether execution produced the expected operational outcome.

Operation Completion

Finalizes the operation and establishes its completed state.

Operation Failure Management

Handles operations that cannot complete successfully and preserves relevant failure state.

Operation State Management

Maintains operational state as the operation progresses through its lifecycle.

Operation Result Management

Produces the internal result of the completed operation for the appropriate infrastructure layer.

MAJOR ROLE 04

Data Query & Retrieval Management

Retrieval is broader than CRUD “read.” Queries can involve conditions, filtering, sorting, pagination, selection, relationships, and subsets of larger datasets.

Query Definition

Represents what data needs to be retrieved and under what conditions.

Query Validation

Determines whether a query is structurally and operationally valid.

Query Preparation

Prepares the query in a form appropriate for the relevant data domain.

Query Execution

Performs the actual query through the appropriate underlying mechanism.

Data Filtering

Restricts retrieved data according to defined criteria.

Data Sorting & Ordering

Controls the order in which retrieved data is presented.

Data Pagination

Allows large datasets to be retrieved in manageable portions.

Data Selection

Determines which records, fields, products, or data elements should be returned.

Data Relationship Retrieval

Retrieves relevant related information when relationships form part of the retrieval requirement.

Query Result Management

Organizes and represents the result produced by the retrieval operation.

MAJOR ROLE 05

Data Integrity & Lifecycle Management

Data is not static. It can change, migrate, synchronize, become concurrently modified, require maintenance, or reach a terminal state. This role protects its trustworthiness across that lifecycle.

Data Integrity Management

Protects the correctness and integrity of managed data.

Data Consistency Management

Ensures that related data remains logically consistent when operations affect multiple pieces of information.

Data Lifecycle Management

Governs data from creation through modification, maintenance, and eventual removal.

Data Migration Management

Manages controlled movement or transformation of data when structures or storage requirements change.

Data Synchronization Management

Maintains required consistency between related data representations or supported sources.

Data Concurrency Management

Handles situations where multiple operations interact with the same data simultaneously.

Data Transaction Management

Coordinates operations that require multiple related changes to succeed or fail together as an appropriate unit.

Data Integrity Verification

Checks whether data remains valid following significant operations or lifecycle events.

Data Maintenance Management

Coordinates maintenance necessary to keep managed data usable and healthy.

Data Lifecycle Completion

Handles the final stage of a data lifecycle, including controlled removal, retirement, or another appropriate terminal state.

The architecture stays understandable because each infrastructure provider owns a different kind of authority.

CORE

Knows the ecosystem.

Core understands providers, capabilities, dependencies, compatibility, lifecycle, configuration, coordination, and ecosystem-level diagnostics. It does not become the data layer.

REQUEST MANAGER

Moves communication.

Request Manager receives, governs, resolves, dispatches, coordinates, completes, responds to, and observes requests. It does not become the data layer.

STORAGE & DATA

Operates on data.

Storage & Data performs supported data operations and manages the associated query, integrity, and lifecycle responsibilities. It does not become the request layer.

08 · Boundary guardrails

What the provider must not become.

“Data” is intentionally bounded. The provider gains responsibilities only when they demonstrably belong to its data domain.

OWNS

Supported data infrastructure

Product data operations, BonikPress-owned data operations, data operation lifecycle, querying/retrieval, and integrity/lifecycle responsibilities.

DOES NOT ABSORB

Other provider domains

Ecosystem registration, provider discovery, request communication, request routing, request lifecycle, UI, task/queue infrastructure, or unrelated domain logic.

CAUTION

Do not equate “stored” with “owned.”

Information being persisted somewhere does not automatically make the Storage & Data Provider the authority over its business meaning.

10 · What comes next

Architecture first. Implementation later.

The current model is deliberately a conceptual skeleton. The next discovery phase should challenge every proposed sub-role rather than treating the first draft as sacred.

NEXT 01

Define every sub-role

Establish exact meaning, purpose, responsibility, ownership boundary, dependencies, and relationships.

NEXT 02

Resolve overlaps

Investigate Product Retrieval vs Query Management, Product Validation vs Operation Validation, and operation verification vs integrity verification.

NEXT 03

Challenge the model

Merge, split, rename, remove, or introduce responsibilities only when architectural evidence supports doing so.

NEXT 04

Only then implement

Classes, interfaces, tables, repositories, APIs, and mechanisms should follow a stable conceptual architecture—not precede it.

The architecture should follow responsibility, not the other way around.
