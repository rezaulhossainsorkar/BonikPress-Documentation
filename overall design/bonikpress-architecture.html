05
Bundles

Bundles contain large plugins only.

A bundle is a curated collection of substantial standalone products that together solve a larger business area.

INVENTORY MANAGEMENT BUNDLE
Inventory Manager
Warehouse Manager
Supplier Manager
Purchase Order Manager
There are no small plugins inside a bundle. The bundle level is reserved for collections of large plugins.
06
Product scale

Different levels of depth.

BonikPress can serve a tiny merchant, a growing business, or a large organization without forcing all of them into the same product.

SMALL

A focused solution to one narrow problem. Simple, accessible, and independently useful.

SUBSTANTIAL

A complete system addressing a significant business requirement with deep functionality.

07
Independent layer

Add-ons

Add-ons are their own section. They are not children of a plugin or bundle. An add-on can extend any compatible BonikPress product, including a large plugin, small plugin, or bundle.

ADD-ON
LARGE PLUGIN
✓ compatible

Core principle: Compatibility is explicit. BonikPress should know what an add-on supports, what it requires, and which combinations are unsupported before installation.

09
Infrastructure

The platform underneath.

The visible catalog stays simple. The infrastructure underneath makes the entire architecture safe, maintainable, and extensible.

✓

Compatibility engine

Product identifiers, supported versions, dependency checks, conflict detection, and installation safeguards.

↗

Extension framework

Stable hooks, APIs, interfaces, events, and extension points for compatible products.

∞

Shared platform services

Licensing, updates, notifications, logging, shared UI, APIs, webhooks, and common services.

11
Final architecture

BonikPress is a platform, not a plugin.

Two ecosystems organize the major business domains. Each ecosystem has Bundles, Large Plugins, and Small Plugins. Add-ons remain independent and connect products only through explicit compatibility.
